Micro-encapsulation ensures slow, targeted release directly inside congested follicles over hours, dislodging cellular debris and bacteria without causing surface peeling or irritation. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use this cleanser both morning and night?

Yes. Because the 2% Salicylic Acid is micro-encapsulated and formulated alongside calming Centella Asiatica, it is gentle enough for twice-daily cleansing without compromising barrier integrity.

Will this product cause purging?

For severely congested skin, mild purging may occur during the first 7-10 days as microscopic clogged microcomedones reach the epidermal surface. This subsides into visibly clarified, smoother skin.

Is this wash safe for pregnancy?

While wash-off salicylic acid products below 2% are generally recognized as low systemic risk, we always advise consulting your primary healthcare provider or obstetrician before introducing active acids.
