## Brand & Style

The brand evokes an intentional, quiet indulgence: sensory, refined, and deeply credible. It addresses discerning consumers seeking high-performance botanical formulations and clinical dermatological care wrapped in an editorial, gallery-like ritual. The visual and emotional tone is calm, tactile, and elevated—communicating purity, meticulous formulation, and timeless beauty rather than fleeting wellness trends.

The design movement is **Warm Minimalist Editorial**. It merges the restrained, generous proportions of high-fashion print publishing with modern transactional fluidity. Interfaces prioritize breathing room, deliberate negative space, precise hairline divisions, and subtle textural contrast between warm stone backgrounds and crisp content surfaces. Every touchpoint feels deliberate, quiet, and architecturally grounded.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in mineral pigments, organic fibers, and precious metals. Rather than sterile optical whites or stark clinical blues, the interface uses layered warm neutrals to establish an atmosphere of serene hospitality.

- **Primary Neutral Surface (`#FAF9F6`)**: The foundation canvas. A warm, linen-inspired tone that reduces eye strain and softens full-bleed compositions.
- **Secondary Canvas & Tonal Backing (`#F3F0EA`)**: Used for page section breaks, filter drawers, cart flyouts, and subtle contrast blocks.
- **Card & Component Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure, crisp paper white reserved for elevated product tiles, dialog modals, and primary cards.
- **Primary Ink (`#171717`)**: Deep charcoal black. Used for high-priority typography, structural iconography, and primary buttons to ground the interface.
- **Secondary & Tertiary Neutral Inks (`#66635F`, `#96918A`)**: Mid-tone warm grays providing accessible contrast for product descriptors, volume weights, timestamps, and placeholder labels.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E3DFD8`)**: A muted alabaster hairline border providing tactile division without visual clutter.
- **Luxury Gold Accent (`#B69A68`)**: A soft, patinated metallic tone reserved for badges, active tier markers, star ratings, subtle hover states, and awards. Never used for large surface fills.
- **Botanical Sage Accent (`#78836F`)**: An earthy, muted herbal green used for clinical claims, clean certification tags, organic ingredient callouts, and positive inventory states.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary grace of **Playfair Display** with the clear, balanced proportions of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Playfair Display** introduces editorial gravitas, poetic product titles, story excerpts, and section headlines. It is used in regular to medium weights with controlled negative letter-spacing for large hero displays to prevent visual dispersion.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides clarity and functional precision across product meta specifications, ingredients, checkout fields, and microcopy. Its slightly open apertures ensure legibility on both mobile touchscreens and ultra-dense desktop tables.
- **Overline / Label Caps**: Subheads, ritual markers, and ingredient categories utilize all-caps tracking (`+0.12em`) set in small, semi-bold sans-serif to create clear structural segmentation without requiring heavy dividers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid system bounded by a centered max width of 1440px, giving high-resolution desktop environments generous side margins that mimic a luxury coffee table book.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, 32px (`2rem`) gutters, and 80px (`5rem`) outer page margins. Section vertical spacing ranges between 72px (`4.5rem`) and 128px (`8rem`) to foster unhurried scanning.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, and 40px (`2.5rem`) outer margins. Multi-column editorial modules collapse into 2-column or 3-column asymmetric pairings.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px)**: 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, and 16px (`1rem`) outer margins. Product carousels transform into horizontal peek snaps with 16px outer overhang.

Vertical flow follows an open rhythm: primary headings preserve generous top margins (`spacing-3xl`) to establish calm thematic transitions between ingredient narratives, clinical trial outcomes, and related rituals.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through soft physical presence, warm tonal contrast, and diffuse atmospheric light rather than harsh drop shadows.

1. **Base Tier (Ground)**: Canvas surfaces (`#FAF9F6` and `#F3F0EA`) remain completely flat and non-elevated.
2. **Structural Tier (Cards & Panels)**: Content surfaces use pure white (`#FFFFFF`) defined by a hairline border (`1px solid #E3DFD8`) coupled with a gentle, tinted ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.03), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.02)`.
3. **Hover & Interactive Tier**: Elevated elements smoothly lift on interaction with an elongated shadow bloom: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.06), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.03)`.
4. **Overlay & Navigational Tier**: Fixed header bars use a translucent warm wash (`rgba(250, 249, 246, 0.85)`) backed by a 12px backdrop blur. Modal dialogs and cart drawers introduce a dimming backdrop overlay of `rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.35)` with an ambient multi-stop shadow: `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, architectural curvature. Elements use restrained 8px (`0.5rem`) to 12px (`0.75rem`) border radii, steering away from blunt square edges as well as overly playful pill shapes.

- **Micro elements (Badges, Checkboxes, Micro-tags)**: 4px radius for crisp readability.
- **Interactive elements (Buttons, Form Inputs, Dropdowns)**: 8px (`0.5rem`) radius, providing a tactile, comfortable edge.
- **Containers (Cards, Modals, Drawer panels)**: 10px to 12px (`0.75rem`) radius, establishing a gentle, bespoke silhouette.
- **Media Framing (Imagery, Hero banners)**: Consistent 8px or 12px outer radius when containerized; full-bleed imagery maintains unrounded boundaries to respect architectural grid lines.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Charcoal (`#171717`) fill, `#FFFFFF` text, 8px border radius, 48px height for mobile/desktop touch targets. Subtle hover transition to `#2E2D2B`.
- **Secondary / Outline**: 1px border (`#E3DFD8`), transparent background, `#171717` text. On hover, background shifts to `#F3F0EA` with border darkening to `#66635F`.
- **Tertiary / Editorial**: Text link styling featuring an underline spaced 4px below the baseline, using `#171717` with smooth transition to `#B69A68`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Editorial Badges (e.g., "Clinical Result", "Award Winner")**: Thin border (`1px solid #E3DFD8`), warm cream background (`#F3F0EA`), typography set to `label-caps` in `#171717` with soft gold dot accent (`#B69A68`).
- **Botanical / Clean Tags**: Tinted sage background (`rgba(120, 131, 111, 0.12)`), `#78836F` text, 4px border radius.
- **Filter Chips**: 32px height, 8px radius, white background (`#FFFFFF`), 1px border (`#E3DFD8`). When selected, fills with Charcoal (`#171717`) and white text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Inputs**: 48px height, `#FFFFFF` surface, 1px border (`#E3DFD8`), 8px radius. Active focus transitions border to `#171717` with no heavy outline rings.
- **Floating Labels**: Set in `label-sm` transitioning smoothly above active input contents using `#66635F`.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes**: 18px x 18px square with 4px border radius. Unselected border `#E3DFD8`. Selected state fills with `#171717` and a white checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons**: 18px circular frame with 1px border (`#E3DFD8`). Selected state displays an inner filled 8px circle in `#171717`.

### Cards & Product Displays
- **Product Tiles**: Crisp white `#FFFFFF` surface, 10px radius, 1px hairline border (`#E3DFD8`). Vertical layout with 4:5 aspect ratio image frame, soft cream background (`#F3F0EA`) within image container, followed by title in `headline-sm`, subtitle/size in `body-sm` (`#66635F`), and price in `label-md`.
- **Editorial Spotlight Cards**: Asymmetric layouts pairing full-bleed ingredient photography with side-by-side text container in `#F3F0EA`.

### Specialized Luxury Skincare Components
- **Ritual Step Counter**: Horizontal numbered indicator (e.g., "01 / CLEANSE", "02 / RESTORE") in `label-caps` with active steps accented in `#B69A68`.
- **Clinical Proof Bar**: Data-driven percentage statistics ("96% noticed refined skin texture") presented in `headline-lg` Playfair Display with supporting study cohort details in `body-sm` (`#66635F`).
